## WM3 entry types

The `entry_type`, `entry_type_label`, `requires_mirror_assessment` and mirror-navigation fields are derived from the Environment Agency's WM3 guidance published on GOV.UK. GOV.UK publishes its Crown copyright content under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) except where otherwise stated. No separate restriction is stated on the WM3 publication page.
